-> Notes <-
- Eye Position measurements in *Meters*
- Origin = (0,0,0) and (looking at the eye) is dead center.
- (X,Y,Z)
    X = To Right
    Y = Up
    Z = Away From Eye
- The left eye center position is (-ipd/2, 0, 0) 
- The right eye center position is (ipd/2, 0, 0)

-> Status <-

- GazeStatus = status in documentation. VALID means eye tracking is OK
                                        INVALID means eye tracking NOT OK
                                        BLANK assume eye tracking NOT OK
- leftEyeStatus/rightEyeStatus = VALID means eye tracking is OK
                  INVALID means eye travcking is NOT OK
                  BLANK assume eye tracking NOT OK

-> What is Invalid??? <-
GazeStatus = INVALID or BLANK
LeftEyeStatus = INVALID or BLANK
RightEyeSTATUS = INVAID or BLANK
